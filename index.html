✧ A LITTLE ABOUT ME ✧

My Journey & Passion

Every insight begins with a question.

Hello, and welcome to my little corner!

I'm Michelle Paul, an MCA graduate from Kerala, India, with a background in Mathematics and Computer Applications.

I completed my B.Sc. in Mathematics at St. Teresa's College, Ernakulam, affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University (MGU). I later pursued my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mar Baselios Institute of Technology and Science (MBITS), Kothamangalam, under APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).

My interest in analytical thinking led me towards data analytics. I enjoy exploring datasets, discovering patterns and presenting information through meaningful visualisations. I've been developing my skills in SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI through practical learning and projects.

Beyond data, I enjoy reading, drawing and exploring creative ideas. I love expressing my imagination, learning new things and seeing the world from different perspectives.

I believe in continuous learning, creativity and taking small steps towards bigger goals. I'm excited to keep growing and explore the possibilities ahead!
